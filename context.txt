WE already have muzzle detection & audio; now optical-flow will decide was the player moving at the moment of the shot. Here’s the stripped-down process I should implement — no fluff.

downscale & mask

Resize to ~360–480p (faster, less noisy).

Apply a fixed mask over HUD/weapon area so UI + gun motion don’t pollute the flow.

stabilize mouse-look (quick)

Find keypoints between consecutive frames (ORB/FAST).

Fit a global affine transform with RANSAC and warp the next frame back to current.

Result: camera rotation/aim changes are mostly removed.

compute optical flow on stabilized frames

Use Farnebäck (cv2.calcOpticalFlowFarneback) on the grayscale, masked pair.

Get the per-pixel flow vectors → compute magnitudes.

reduce to one number per frame

Take a robust statistic from the magnitude map (e.g., median or 90th percentile ignoring masked pixels). That single number is your “motion score.”

smooth + debounce

Smooth the motion score with an exponential moving average over ~0.5–1s to remove spikes. (EMA α ≈ 0.2–0.4)

Require the motion score to be above/below threshold for N frames (6–10 frames) before flipping the state to avoid flicker.

auto-calibrate thresholds (easy)

In the first 2–3 seconds of a clip, compute baseline median and MAD of the motion score.

Set idle_threshold ≈ baseline_median + 1×MAD and move_threshold ≈ baseline_median + 3×MAD.

If motion_score < idle_threshold → still; else → moving.

tie to the shot event (use your muzzle detection)

When you detect a muzzle flash (or bullet sound), sample the smoothed motion score at that timestamp (or average across ±0.25s).

If score < idle_threshold → player standing still while shooting. Else → shooting while moving.

Quick practical tips

Skip every other frame if CPU is tight (compute flow at e.g., 15–20 fps).

Visualize the motion score timeline once (plot) — you’ll instantly see where thresholds should sit.

Weapon/hand region may still wiggle after stabilization — make sure your HUD/weapon mask covers it.

If mistakes persist, raise mask or add a tiny YOLOv8-tiny to explicitly detect weapon area and exclude it.
